Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 8, 2026

Important Notice: Aurader is an intelligent ebook reader application designed to provide a premium reading and listening experience. We highly value your privacy protection. All book processing and text-to-speech generation is performed locally on your device or using on-device engines. We do not upload, store, or share your personal files with any third party.

1. Information We Collect

The types of information we collect include:

  • Required Information:
    • Account Information: Apple ID (for subscription management only)
    • Device Information: device model, operating system version (for app functionality)
    • App Usage Data: reading time, feature usage frequency (for service improvement)
    • Crash Reports: technical data related to app crashes (for issue resolution)
  • File Access:
    • Book Files: We access the EPUB, PDF, and other document files you explicitly import into the app.
    • Reading Progress: We store your bookmarks, highlights, and reading position locally.
    • We do not access any other files on your device without your permission.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide ebook reading and text-to-speech functionality
  • Manage your local library of imported books
  • Save your reading progress, bookmarks, and highlights
  • Process your subscription payments (through Apple's payment system)
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Respond to your inquiries and requests

3. Data Storage and Processing

Regarding data processing, please note:

  • All book file parsing is performed locally on your device
  • Imported books are stored locally in the app's sandbox
  • We do not upload your book files to any server
  • Reading data (progress, highlights) is stored locally
  • All local data is deleted upon app uninstallation
  • Analytics data uses Apple's privacy-preserving analytics service

4. Book Library Access

Our app requires access to your files to:

  • Import ebooks (EPUB, PDF, etc.) from compliance sources
  • Display book covers and metadata
  • Analyze text for Text-to-Speech playback
  • You retain full ownership and control of your imported files

5. Subscription Services

Regarding subscriptions:

  • Payment will be charged to your iTunes account
  • Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your device's Settings → iTunes & App Store → Apple ID → Subscriptions
  • Any unused portion of a free trial period will be forfeited when purchasing a subscription

6. Data Security

We implement the following measures to protect your data:

  • Local data processing without cloud uploads
  • Secure file handling mechanisms
  • App sandbox isolation for imported books
  • Regular security reviews
  • Strict internal access controls

7. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for users aged 4 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us for deletion.

8. Your Rights

You have the following rights:

  • Access your books and reading data stored in the app
  • Delete your imported books at any time
  • Clear reading history and cache data
  • Opt-out of analytics data collection (via system settings)

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will notify you in the app and update the "Last Updated" date on this page. Your continued use of our services after such changes indicates your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

10. App Store Data Usage and Privacy

Our application complies with Apple's App Store privacy label requirements. You can view detailed data collection and usage information on our App Store page.
